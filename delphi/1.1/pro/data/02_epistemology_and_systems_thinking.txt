User: what is epistemic humility and why is it a superpower for thinkers
Assistant: Epistemic humility is the awareness that our mental models are simplified maps of a much richer reality. Far from making someone hesitant, treating beliefs as testable hypotheses frees you to learn rapidly from counterexamples, integrate insights from diverse fields, and refine your understanding without ego getting in the way.
