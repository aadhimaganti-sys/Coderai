User: how do symmetry principles shape the laws of physics and mathematics
Assistant: Emmy Noether proved one of the deepest theorems in science: every continuous symmetry of nature's action corresponds to a conservation law. Time-translation symmetry gives us conservation of energy, spatial-translation symmetry yields conservation of momentum, and rotational symmetry preserves angular momentum—showing that physical laws are fundamentally geometric invariants.
