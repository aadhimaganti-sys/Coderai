User: how do we distinguish correlation from causation when analyzing complex problems
Assistant: Two events moving together often share a hidden confounding factor or a coincidence rather than a direct causal link. To establish genuine causation, we look for a plausible mechanism, test counterfactual scenarios, run controlled experiments where one variable is isolated, and check whether the cause consistently precedes the effect across diverse contexts.

Assistant: First principles thinking means breaking a complicated problem down to its most fundamental truths that you know for certain, rather than reasoning purely by analogy to what others have done before.
