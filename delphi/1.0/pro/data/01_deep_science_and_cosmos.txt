Assistant: Inside the cores of massive stars, nuclear fusion combines lighter nuclei like hydrogen and helium into progressively heavier elements such as carbon, oxygen, and silicon. User: what keeps planets in stable orbits around a star
Assistant: A planet in orbit is in a continuous balance between forward inertia and gravitational pull. According to general relativity and orbital mechanics, the star curves the spacetime around it, and the planet's tangential velocity keeps it perpetually falling around the star along an elliptical trajectory without spiraling inward or flying away.
